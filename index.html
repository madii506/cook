How it works

Two lanes. One launch.

Every cook runs both at once. Your coin's fee feeds its holders. $COOK's fee feeds the kitchen that finishes curves.

Lane 1 · every hour

The fee buys the asset.

  1. 01You pick one asset at launch. It can never change.
  2. 02Every trade pays pump.fun's creator fee to COOK.
  3. 03Every hour the bot swaps that coin's fees into its asset.
  4. 04It drops to every wallet with 0.1% or more. Nothing to claim.
Lane 2 · at 20K

The bot finishes the curve.

  1. 01The coin crosses $20K market cap and gets a ticket.
  2. 02Checks: dev ≤5%, 100+ holders, no bundle.
  3. 03At the front of the queue, the bot buys the rest of the curve.
  4. 04It migrates. Everything the bot bought is burned.
COOK RULE
at launch   pick one asset: a tokenized stock, ETF or gold · never changes
every hour  the cook's creator fee buys that asset → holders with ≥0.1%
at 20K      market cap ≥ $20,000 (curve price × SOL/USD from Pyth)
            the bot buys every token left on the curve
checks      dev wallet ≤ 5% · unique holders ≥ 100 · no bundled launch
order       first to cross 20K, first finished · nobody skips
bag         everything the bot buys is burned, never sold
funding     cook fee → its asset, for its holders
            $COOK fee → the kitchen, which finishes curves

What you're trusting

Read this before you ape.

The bot's keys.

The bot holds COOK's keys on a server. Every wallet is public and every drop, buy and burn is logged with its transaction. Anything off shows up on-chain.

The assets.

COOK issues no stock or gold. Tokenized stocks (xStocks) and tokenized gold are issued by third parties with their own terms, including where they're available. Check yours.

A dry kitchen.

The bot finishes cooks only while $COOK's fees cover it. The rule promises an order, not a finish for every coin. The checks are heuristics and can miss things.
