FAQ

Questions from the pass.

What is COOK?

A pump.fun launchpad. Every coin launched on it is tied to one real asset, and its creator fee buys that asset for holders.

What can my coin pay in?

Tokenized stocks and ETFs (xStocks like NVDA, TSLA, SPY) and tokenized gold. You pick one at launch. It can never change.

How do holders get paid?

Every hour the bot swaps that coin's creator fee into its asset and sends it to every wallet holding 0.1% or more, split by size. Nothing to claim.

What is the 20K bot?

When a cook crosses $20K market cap, the bot buys the rest of its bonding curve so it migrates. Coins are finished in the order they hit 20K.

Who pays for the bot?

$COOK does. Its own creator fee fills the kitchen wallet that finishes curves. Cook fees never go to the kitchen.

What happens to the tokens the bot buys?

They are burned right after the buy. The bot never sells.

Can a dev farm the bot?

Every coin is checked at 20K and again before the buy: dev wallet 5% or less, 100+ holders, no bundled launch. Fail one and the ticket is pulled.

What if the kitchen runs dry?

The queue waits. Nobody skips, not even us. The rule promises an order, not a finish for every coin.

Are these real stocks?

They are tokenized versions issued by third parties with their own terms and country limits. COOK issues none of them. Check they are available where you live.

Where is the CA?

$COOK is not live yet. The only CA is the one this page and the COOK account will post at launch. Anything posted before that is not ours.

Can I check any of this myself?

Yes. The coin checker above reads real pump.fun curves from Solana right now. After launch, every drop, buy and burn is logged with its transaction.
